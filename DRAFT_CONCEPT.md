Game Concept: The Ghost ArchitectCore Hook: You aren't just defending; you're investigating a breach in real-time.
The game uses a mock OS interface (similar to Emily is Away or Her Story) to make the browser feel like a high-stakes workstation.Phase 1: The Onboarding (Tutorial)The Setting: You log into the "Nexus Corp" employee portal.The Task: A simple security audit. You’re asked to check emails, verify software updates, and set up MFA.The Twist: You receive a DM from a "manager" asking you to bypass a protocol "just this once" to meet a deadline.Learning Goal: Recognizing Social Engineering and the "Urgency" tactic.Phase 2: The Breach (Core Gameplay)The screen glitches. An alert flashes: Unauthorized Access Detected. The game shifts into a "Defend & Trace" mode.Email Triage: You must sort through an inbox of 10 emails. Some are legit; others are sophisticated phishing attempts. Identifying the "red flags" (mismatched URLs, suspicious attachments) grants you "Firewall Points."The Password Puzzle: You find a compromised account. You must "harden" it by selecting the strongest password from a list, demonstrating why $P@ssw0rd123$ is useless compared to long passphrases.The Wi-Fi Trap: Your "character" goes to a coffee shop. You must choose between a "Free Public Wi-Fi" and a secure VPN. Choosing incorrectly leads to a "Man-in-the-Middle" simulation where you see your data being scraped in real-time.Phase 3: The Investigation (Analysis)You’ve stopped the bleed, but now you must find the source.Log Analysis: You look at a simplified server log. You have to spot the IP address that doesn't belong or the "odd hour" login.Malware Cleanup: A mini-game where you identify "Suspect Files" based on extensions (e.g., invoice.pdf.exe vs invoice.pdf).Phase 4: The Debrief (Outcome)Based on your choices, you receive a "Security Clearance Score."High Score: You are promoted to Chief Security Officer.Low Score: You are "fired," and the screen shows a mock news report of the company’s data being sold on the dark web.Technical Flow (The Browser Experience)ComponentTechnologyPurposeInterfaceHTML5 / CSS3Creates a fake "Desktop" environment within the browser.LogicJavaScript (React/Vue)Handles the branching narrative and point scoring.State ManagementLocalStorageAllows players to "save" their progress in the browser session.VisualsSVG/CanvasUsed for "hacking" mini-games and terminal simulations.Key Awareness PillarsTo ensure the game is educational, each level should map to one of these real-world concepts:Phishing/Vishing: Not clicking suspicious links.MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication): Its importance in stopping credential theft.Physical Security: Not plugging in "found" USB drives.Software Hygiene: The danger of "Shadow IT" (using unauthorized apps for work).Pro Tip: Keep the "Hacking" visual. Instead of complex code, use "Pattern Recognition." It makes the player feel smart without requiring a Computer Science degree.
